SNAPSHOT ANCHORING (REST snapshot id vs websocket diff id):
  On fifty-six symbol-day samples the two id spaces
  are FOREIGN to each other: the snapshot median id runs about
  twenty-six times ahead of the diff median id, with zero
  overlap — snapshots cannot anchor diffs on those days.
  On four samples (the day the UPGRADED recorder
  finally took effect on a restart) the spaces interleave with a
  ratio of one to one — from that day forward, full-book
  reconstruction (snapshot anchor plus diffs) is possible on the
  forward tape. The per-day detail is in the JSON.

Interpretation: through most of the recorded window the websocket
diff tape ran in a foreign id space — the mirror-host pathology the
recorder docstring documents (the deployed keepalive kept the OLD
recording code alive across restarts), while REST snapshots live in
the canonical fapi space. The bookTicker top-of-book tape is
independent of any snapshot space, which is why the fill-replay
adapter resolves fills on it.

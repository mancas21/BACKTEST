# 9-21 EMA crossover: all pairs, all time frames

Strategy: the attached `ema_9_21_backtest-claude.py`, ported into the harness
unchanged (EMA 9/21 crossover, next-open entry, 10-bar swing stop, 2R target,
slow-EMA cross exit, 1% risk, 5x cap), then run over every pair and every
time frame present in `data/`. The port adds only what this repo requires:
injected costs (5 bps fee + 0.5 bps slippage per side, 11 bps round trip),
**funding charged at the real settlement instants**, the window trimmed to
the funded span, and the accounting identity `eq[-1] - INIT == sum(pnl)`
asserted inside every artifact.

Sources of record: `code/ema921_source.py` (byte-identical to the
attachment, sha256 `8be0020d057a4ef73fbed9e4ab4f11b97d131f3d62b3094f47e6ded80a2189c4`)
and `code/ema921_engine.py`. Audit `code/_ema921_audit.py` (all checks
pass: known answers for entry, stop, target, fees, sizing, SL-first ties,
gap fills, funding sign/timing, causality, determinism), parity
`code/_ema921_parity.py` (all four parity cells bit-identical to the source
under the source's own 4/1 bps costs with funding off).

Every cell below is an artifact under `results/runs/` that re-runs
bit-for-bit (`vrun.py pverify`: all one hundred and one OK).

## Universe

One hundred and one (symbol, timeframe) cells: eight at 1m, ten at 5m,
thirty-five at 15m, thirty-seven at 1h and eleven at 1d. Native kline
directories are preferred, the taker cache is the fallback (JUP at 1m,
XRP at 5m, most recent listings at 15m/1h). Deliberately excluded, with
reasons: `SOLUSDC` (a spot pair with no funding file), the 30-day 5m stub
in `data/binance_klines/` (the real history is `data/binance_klines_5m/`),
the `_tail_` appendices and `.preflight.tmp` leftovers (not continuous
series), and every non-USDT file.

Funding is enforced, not optional: every cell bills the real settlements
(rate x notional at the bar that contains the settlement, before any fill
at that open), the window is trimmed to the funded span at both ends, and
`funding_covers_bars` is true in every artifact. The largest hole in a
funding file inside its window is PUMPUSDT at 15m (`d1f3698c3c22f018`, `funding_max_gap_h` 504.00 hours), a known
exchange-data hole, published rather than hidden.

## Verdict

**Zero cells pass the repo battery.** The battery is: n >= 30, PF > 1.2,
net > 0, OOS PF > 1.2 and OOS net > 0 on a 70/30 chronological split,
green in at least two of three periods, best-five concentration <= 100%
with a positive net without them, a bootstrap CI of the mean trade above
zero, and a return above buy & hold.

Only two cells even reach a full-window PF above 1.2 with enough trades,
and both die on significance and on buy & hold:

* **LABUSDT 1h** `dfab81a68433a1fd`: net 28.44%, PF 1.270, OOS PF 1.558, green in 3 of 3 thirds, but the bootstrap CI of the mean trade is [-1.950, 18.696] -- it includes zero (P(mean<=0) = 0.059). It only beats buy & hold (-83.22%) because the token collapsed; winning by losing less is not alpha.
* **ZECUSDT 1h** `d620f41a9ece082a`: net 33.06%, PF 1.262, OOS PF 1.324, green in 3 of 3 thirds, but the CI is [-1.297, 18.243] (P(mean<=0) = 0.047) and buy & hold made 2884.79% against its 33.06%.

Both are recent listings: their windows start in 2025, so a single
regime is all the evidence they have.

## Why the rest die

**1m and 5m: no gross edge at all.** The zero-cost control is the
strategy with fees and slippage set to zero on the same window. At 1m
only one of the eight cells is above 1.0 even before costs (SPXUSDT 1m `67d8a9a9cd843510`, zero-cost PF 1.022), and the best 1m cell on
costs and funding, ETHUSDT 1m `1e231a62c3ecc4d9`, still
loses the account with PF 0.134 on 83475 trades. At 5m six of
ten are above 1.0 before costs but none reaches 1.2, so costs cannot be
blamed alone.

**15m and 1h: a real but tiny gross edge, destroyed by turnover.** At 15m
fifteen of thirty-five cells are above 1.0 before costs and none reaches
1.2; at 1h twenty-five of thirty-seven are above 1.0 before costs and five
reach 1.2. The fee bill explains the gap: BTCUSDT 1h `61da2863c36f44da` paid 14588.02 USDT in fees on 2812 trades (zero-cost PF 1.082 versus 0.891 with costs).
SOLUSDT 15m `abc036cc36e2849c` paid 39109.08 USDT. The strategy trades a
crossover every few dozen bars at up to 5x notional; at 11 bps per round
trip the fees compound faster than any 2R target can.

**1d: no verdict, not a win.** The daily files start in 2026 by
construction, so no daily cell reaches thirty trades; several show a
pretty profit factor on a handful of trades, which is noise by the repo's
own sample floor. They are marked INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE and are not
evidence either way.

**Funding is real but small here.** Charging it at the settlements moves
the cells by at most a couple of points: the largest drag in the grid is
AVAXUSDT 1h `24b242051b5957a7` with `funding_drag_pp` -1.97 (it paid 246.45 USDT), and some shorts
receive funding instead (LABUSDT 1h `dfab81a68433a1fd`, `funding_paid` -11.99). The
funding regime never rescues a cell that loses before it, and never
breaks one that wins.

## Auditor checklist (the eight usual killers)

* **Look-ahead: OK.** Signals are evaluated on the close and filled at
  the next open; the audit truncates the series and the earlier equity
  path is bit-identical, and the parity run matches the source trade by
  trade.
* **Survivorship: OK by construction.** The universe is every file on
  disk, including delisted and collapsed tokens (LABUSDT and SIRENUSDT
  are in the table, and buy & hold loses most of its value in several
  cells), not a today-listed screen.
* **Missing costs: fixed.** 11 bps round trip per leg, and funding
  charged at the real settlement instants on the notional held.
* **Overfitting: not applicable to the headline, and damning where it
  applies.** No parameter was tuned: the source's defaults are used for
  every cell. The two cells above 1.2 are therefore the maximum of a
  large grid, which is exactly what the bootstrap CI is there to punish.
* **Out-of-sample: measured, and failed.** The 70/30 split is in every
  artifact; both near-miss cells pass it, the significance gate kills
  them.
* **Too few trades: flagged.** Every 1d cell is under thirty trades and
  marked; every other cell is above it.
* **Outliers: measured.** `conc_net_without_top5_pct` removes the five
  best trades; no cell's result depends on them in the passing
  direction.
* **Fills: conservative.** Stop-first on ties, gaps filled at the open,
  targets at the limit price, slippage on every market fill. The audit
  tests all of it in both directions.

## Caveats stated plainly

* `NVDAUSDT` and `SPCXUSDT` are repo instruments, not Binance USD-M
  perpetuals; their funding files are short (a few hundred settlements)
  and are charged as they are, but their provenance is weaker than the
  crypto perps'. Their cells are not evidence about a listed perp.
* The 1m files cover 2023 onward only; the 1d files start in 2026. Each
  row prints the window it actually traded.
* The leverage cap binds in the fast time frames (5x gross on several 1m
  and 5m cells), so those returns are the maximum the account could
  express, not a conservative reading.
* The source's own defaults are 4/1 bps per side and no funding; the
  parity check runs those numbers and matches bit-for-bit. The headline
  numbers here are the repo's 5/0.5 bps plus funding, as required.

## Reproduce

```
python code/_ema921_audit.py          # known answers, must pass
python code/_ema921_parity.py         # port == source, bit-for-bit
python code/_ema921_matrix.py --workers 4
python code/vrun.py pverify --ids <the ids in _matrix_ema921.csv> --workers 4
python code/vrun.py verify <run_id>   # any single cell
python code/vrun.py stats <run_id>    # institutional scorecard
```

Each artifact is `results/runs/<run_id>.json`; its `core` binds the kline
and funding bytes, the engine sha256 and the data cut, so an edit to any
of them makes the artifact STALE instead of silently different.

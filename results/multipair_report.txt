Multi-Pair Crypto Strategy Study
20 long/cash strategies | BTC/USDC, ETH/USDC, SOL/USDC, AVAX/USDC, LINK/USDC | Hourly data | 2026-03-26 to 2026-09-26 UTC
Research contract. $100,000 initial capital; common five-pair Binance Spot universe; signals at a completed hourly close and execution at the
following open. Each execution uses 0.05% taker fee and 0.02% adverse slippage per side. Long/cash only: no synthetic spot shorts or omitted
funding. Protective stops use each strategy's ATR(14) multiple. Targets are capped at 35% per pair, 100% gross, 1% planned stop risk per name,
4% aggregate stop heat, with a 50% gross cap on selected correlation clusters containing an absolute 720h correlation of at least 0.80. Sharpe and
Sortino use daily UTC equity returns (0% risk-free rate).
Data validation. Public Binance Spot REST candles; five USDC pairs, each with 5,137 contiguous hourly rows: 720 warm-up hours from
2026-02-24, 4,416 evaluation hours, and one boundary execution bar. Dataset retrieval metadata and SHA-256 file inventory are retained in the
research folder.

Definitions & limitations
Cumulative return is terminal equity / $100,000 - 1 after forced terminal liquidation. Profit factor is gross profitable trade P&L; divided by absolute
gross losing P&L.; Expectancy R is mean trade P&L; divided by its initial planned stop risk. Trade count is closed position episodes, including
terminal closes. Results are an in-sample historical simulation, not investment advice; hourly OHLC cannot establish fill priority when a bar contains
both a stop and other unobserved intrabar events.
Source: Binance Spot public market-data REST API, retrieved 2026-09-26. Method, source URLs, timestamps, data validation, strategy parameters,
metrics, and per-strategy CSV/SVG artifacts are retained alongside this report.

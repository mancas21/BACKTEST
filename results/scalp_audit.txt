GATE 2 — TESTED (45 months, 5 symbols, ~9.9M 1m bars)

SCALP-4 funding squeeze: ZERO trades on all 5 symbols.
  |z|>2 funding plus a 1m reversal candle never coincided.
  Arithmetically empty, not merely unprofitable.

SCALP-5 / SCALP-8 sweep + tickvol: ACCOUNT LIQUIDATED on all
  5 symbols. The spec's own sizing rule is the cause: '1.0-1.2%
  portfolio risk per trade' against a 0.15% stop implies
  0.012/0.0015 = 8x notional. Measured implied leverage ran
  24x to 238x depending on symbol and equity path. A stop-
  based risk target and a 0.15% stop are in direct tension.

SCALP-10 nonlinear model (logistic + gradient boosting):
  logistic accuracy 0.515 / 0.519 / 0.518 / 0.518 / 0.516
  on BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP/SUI. A coin toss. The GBM produced ZERO
  signals past its own 70% confidence gate because no model
  reaches that confidence on 1m data. An Informer with GMADL
  would find what logistic cannot only by overfitting harder.

CONCLUSION
  1-minute scalping is not a retail activity. 7bps of round-trip
  cost against a 23bps minute-ATR means costs are ~30% of the
  entire bar range. The edge would have to be enormous before
  fees enter the conversation. The source document's own
  docstring states its Sharpe figures are 'illustrative
  specifications derived from cited research, not fabricated
  backtests' — the ranking table has no strategy behind it.

FUNDING COVERAGE CHECK — datacheck_funding.py
Exact asof_utc, sha256, spans and every exact count: data/FUNDING_MANIFEST.json.
This text carries findings in words only (nothing below is a backtest
measurement, so no run_id is needed behind it).

Scope: eight funding files in data/binance_funding/, cross-checked against every kline CSV of the same symbol in data/binance_klines*/.
Findings:
  - Settlement series: seven files have missing settlement
    prints and/or duplicate or non-monotonic timestamps (per-file detail
    in the manifest; missing prints mean bars inside those intervals
    carried no funding charge).
  - Interval switches: one files contain stretches at a
    shorter funding interval than their modal one (e.g. an exchange moving
    from eight-hour to four-hour funding). Benign for billing — every
    settlement is present — but any consumer assuming a fixed interval
    should read modal_spacing_h and the switch counts in the manifest.
  - Coverage vs bars: of fifty-five funding-file x kline-window pairs, fifty-three are fully covered (settlement span reaches both
    ends of the bar window); the rest carry head_gap/tail_gap tags in the
    manifest. A head_gap means the earliest bars were charged NO funding;
    a tail_gap means the latest bars were charged none.
    RESULT: three pairs are NOT fully covered — see manifest
    field coverage[].issues before trusting any funding-sensitive number
    built on those bars.

Manifest: data/FUNDING_MANIFEST.json (snapshot; `vrun stale` is the
authority if data bytes change after the snapshot moment).

# Audit of the two attached documents

Scope: `Downloads/STRATEGY_GENERATOR_PROMPT-GLM5.3.md` (the generator prompt,
v1.0, dated 2026-10-27) and `Downloads/strategy_candidates_v2-claude.md` (seven
candidate strategies, Batch v2.0, same date). Method: every factual claim was
checked against the repository's own data, code and persisted artifacts. Every
number below is read from an artifact cited in this file; every artifact was
re-run by `vrun.py pverify` and reproduces bit-for-bit. Where a claim could not
be measured, it says so instead of estimating.

The candidate document is answered by the run itself: `results/cand7_report.md`
(seven artifacts, `guard` clean). This file is the document-level audit — what
the two documents get right, what they get wrong, and the exact corrections.

---

## Part 1 — the generator prompt

### 1.1 What checks out

- **The data block is accurate.** Seven taker symbols (BTC, ETH, SOL, HYPE, SUI,
  JUP, XRP) at 1m / 5m / 15m / 1h, plain OHLCV only for SPXUSDT, funding history
  for every symbol, about two years from October 2024. HYPE and JUP are shorter,
  as stated.
- **The cost arithmetic is right.** Five bps fee plus two bps slippage per side
  is fourteen bps round trip; the repo gate block is eleven bps round trip.
- **The gate thresholds match the code.** `code/gate2.py` POLICY carries the
  hundred-trade floor, the thirty-trade out-of-sample floor, five folds with a
  seventy-percent pass fraction, sixty percent green months with a minus-fifteen
  percent worst month, a thirty-five percent P95 drawdown cap, and a strict
  positive expectancy lower bound. The prompt's numbers are the same numbers.
- **The null-pass calibration is right.** `results/gate_calibration_report.md`
  puts the null pass rate at or below a quarter of one percent per cell.
- **The near-miss figures are right.** P28 is an ETHUSDT 5m momentum-ignition
  book, artifact `ff1403fda59140c8`: profit factor 1.3156 on 266 trades,
  breakeven 14.26 bps per side, expectancy lower bound -0.568 bps.
- **The one full pass is described correctly.** The delta-neutral XRP grid,
  artifact `8bf3575619eda66f`: 86.8108% win rate, 5876 trades, profit factor
  1.498, three green calendar years.

### 1.2 What is wrong

**F1 — The prompt kills the family it recommends.** Section 3 lists
"momentum-ignition copies" among the measured dead. Section 4 then presents an
ETH 5m momentum-ignition book as "the closest any book has come (measured)" —
P28, `ff1403fda59140c8`. A generator reading section 3 will discard the exact
design section 4 tells it to imitate. The two statements can be reconciled only
by a distinction the prompt never draws: the family is dead as a class at
1m/3m and without the session and regime gates; the 5m ETH variant with those
gates is the near-miss.

*Correction (section 3):* replace `momentum-ignition copies,` with
`momentum-ignition copies below 15m and without a higher-timeframe regime gate
(see section 4 for the one 5m variant that came close),`.

**F2 — "Weekend/session gap fills on 1h bars repeatedly reached PF 1.2+" is
overstated.** Two such books exist on disk. S46 (weekend-gap fade, BTC 1h,
`be0a85f7ab2b522c`) reaches 1.3028 and fails concentration. P44 (weekend gap
fade, SUI 1h, `73ec7766d0e92513`) reaches 1.1885 and fails the profit-factor
gate. That is one book above the threshold, not a repeated result.

*Correction:* replace the bullet with
`Weekend/session gap fills on 1h bars are the only calendar family with a book
above PF 1.2 so far (S46, 1.3028, killed by concentration; P44 next at 1.1885).`

**F3 — "33 of 42 such books" borrows a denominator from a different pack.** The
thirty-three ruined books and the forty-two runnable prompts are both from the
Qwen pack (`results/pack50c_report.md`), and the forty-two includes books that
are not 1m/3m scalps. The sentence reads as if forty-two scalping books were
tested; they were not.

*Correction:* `every 1m/3m scalping book (in the Qwen pack, thirty-three of the
forty-two runnable books ended the window ruined).`

**F4 — "roughly fifteen hundred strategies" counts runs, not strategies.** The artifact store
holds roughly fifteen hundred runs across twenty-six strategy keys, with many
runs per strategy (timeframes, symbols, cost variants, re-runs after engine
fixes). The multiple-testing haircut is real, but the honest unit is the run.

*Correction:* `about fifteen hundred backtest runs have already been scored in
this pipeline.`

**F5 — The G8 cost claim is stale.** The prompt says a strategy "must remain
profitable at 22 bps round trip to pass G8". G8 as implemented re-runs the book
at twice its own declared per-side cost; for the pack's fourteen-bps block that
is a stress well above twenty-two bps. P28's artifact records the stressed
factor (`pf_at_2x_cost` 1.1226), which is how the gate is actually scored. The
twenty-two-bps preset survives only as the legacy sensitivity in
`AGENTS.md`.

*Correction:* `G8 re-runs the book at twice its declared per-side cost (the
legacy twenty-two-bps preset remains as a sensitivity); a strategy must stay
above PF 1 there and keep costs under thirty-five percent of gross profit.`

**F6 — "the pack never tested this family" is false at the repository level.**
Cross-sectional rotation was tested: ds4 in the DeepSeek pack is a
thirteen-symbol cross-sectional top-three book, ported and killed
(`results/dspack_report.md`). It was not tested in the four 50-prompt packs,
which is a narrower and less useful statement.

*Correction:* `Cross-sectional rotation: rank the 7 perps ... (tested once as
ds4 on thirteen symbols and killed; not tested in the 50-prompt packs).`

**F7 — The near-miss is presented without its failures.** P28 fails the halves
gate (its out-of-sample factor is 1.00) and five of the eight v2 gates.
The prompt quotes only the expectancy shortfall, which makes the book look one
basis point from passing when it is one basis point from passing *one* gate.

*Correction:* append to the near-miss paragraph: `It still fails the halves gate
and five of the eight v2 gates; it is a template for the shape of an edge, not a
strategy that almost passed.`

**F8 — Minor.** "XRP also at 15m/1m" implies the other six are not; all seven
have all four taker timeframes. The trade-count floor is quoted as a hundred
(the v2 floor) while the packs were graded at thirty (the v1 floor); the prompt
should say which set it means.

### 1.3 What the prompt gets right and should keep

The mechanism requirement, the explicit-numbers requirement, the concentration
self-check, the "declare data fears" rule and the refusal to accept generated
performance figures are the parts that made the candidate batch auditable. The
seven candidates that came out of it were specific enough to port without a
single clarifying question, which is not true of the earlier packs.

### 2.2 Answers to the document's own questions (section D)

**D1 — coverage.** All seven taker series begin on 2024-10-14. BTC and SOL run
to 2026-10-26, ETH to 2026-10-14, JUP to 2026-10-15; HYPE starts in mid-2025.
The artifacts record zero missing bars and no gap breach for every series used.
Funding exists for every symbol; JUP's was incomplete when the batch started and
was fetched to full coverage before the run.

**D2 — taker-buy aggregation.** Binance publishes taker-buy base volume per
bar. On resampling, taker-buy base and total volume are summed independently
and the ratio is recomputed; the ratio is never averaged. C1 uses the 6-bar sum
form the document specifies.

**D3 — cost convention.** Both repo numbers are round-trip totals: eleven bps
is five bps fee plus half a bp slippage per side; twenty-two bps is the legacy
six-plus-five per side. The document's own block is five plus two per side,
fourteen bps round trip, which sits between them. Running at the document's
block is therefore conservative relative to the repo gate, and the scoreboard
carries the repo-cost factor beside it.

**D4 — event counts.** First-crossing and absorption signals, measured in the
artifacts: C1 306, C3 38, C6 14, C7 54, C2 12. Overlap with the killed ETH
ignition events was **not** measured; that join does not exist in any artifact
and is not estimated here.

**D5 — event studies against matched controls.** Not run. The batch went
straight to strategy-level backtests, which is the reverse of the order the
document asked for. The results are still valid as a screen (a book with no
gross edge cannot survive a matched-control study), but the three books with a
gross edge would have been better served by the event study first.

**D6 — DST.** C3 anchors its signal to fixed UTC hours, so the "US-hours
handoff" label is approximate for the months when New York is on daylight time.
The result was not split by DST period; with 38 trades a split would have two
samples too small to read.

**D7 — stop/target co-occurrence.** C2's artifact records zero bars where the
stop and the target were both touched (12 trades). The tie rule never fired, so
the stop-first convention did not decide this result.

**D8 — funding.** The SOL-minus-BTC three-settlement differential was at or
below minus 2.5 bps in 0.0689 of settlements and at or above plus 2.5 bps in
0.0279. Settlements are eight-hourly for both legs in this window. That
asymmetry is why C6's symmetric design produced fourteen long-SOL signals and
zero short-SOL signals.

**D9 — beta and dispersion.** C4's realised beta is -0.1043 with a median
cross-sectional dispersion of 1.3213; C5's beta is 0.0001 with a median
dispersion of 3.0833 over the three days it traded. C5's dispersion figure is
computed only on accepted days, so it describes the gate's own tail, not the
universe.

### 2.3 Where the document was wrong

**C4's book kill cannot bind.** Four legs at an eight-percent stop on a
half-gross book cap the period loss well under the fifteen-percent kill; it
fired zero times in 90 traded periods. A control that cannot act is a design
defect, not a risk control.

**C5's gate is in the wrong units.** The dispersion gate asks for a 3.0 spread
in vol-scaled 24-bar returns. It fired on three of 699 days. The candidate is
untestable as written; that is a finding about the specification, not the tape.

**C6 was one-sided in this window.** Fourteen signals, all long SOL / short
BTC. The document presents the pair as symmetric; the data made it directional,
and the funding credit did not cover the spread loss.

**C2's stop is tighter than its sample.** A quarter-ATR stop with twelve trades
and an expectancy lower bound of -8.38 bps: the gross factor is real (1.7765)
and the breakeven is 28.42 bps per side, but twelve observations cannot
establish the sign. The document's preflight flagged frequency as unresolved,
and frequency is what killed it.

**C7 dies on five trades.** The five best trades are 191.49% of total PnL;
without them the book is -2.27%. The v2 top-five-percent gate passes, the v1
top-five-trades gate fails, and the v1 gate is the stricter one here.

**Section C's "SPCX is unverified" is stale.** SPXUSDT is on disk at 1m and 1h
with funding; what is unverified is that it supports taker-flow designs, because
its files carry no taker-buy field and its 1m window is about three months.

## Part 3 — what changed in the repository

- `code/cand7_core.py`, `code/cand7_strats.py`, `code/cand7_engine.py`: the
  seven-candidate engine, with the declared rules stored in each artifact.
- `code/_cand7_audit.py`: twenty-seven known-answer checks. It caught four
  defects before publication (basket rows missing fees and funding; the pair
  book marking both legs long; time exits labelled as signal exits; C4
  flattening instead of skipping a low-dispersion rebalance).
- `code/_cand7_assemble_report.py`: builds `results/cand7_report.md` from the
  artifacts, so the prose cannot drift from the numbers.
- `code/vrun.py`: adapter `cand7`.
- Seven artifacts under `results/runs/`, all `pverify` clean.

The generator prompt's original is **not** edited in place. The corrections from
section 1.2 (findings F1 to F8, nine replacements because F4 and F8 each carry
two) are applied by `code/_fix_generator_prompt.py`, which asserts that every
replacement matches exactly once and writes a copy to
`results/STRATEGY_GENERATOR_PROMPT-GLM5.3_corrected.md`. The build script fails
loudly rather than silently skipping a replacement. The copy differs from the
original on nine passages; line lengths stay within the original's seventy-nine
column wrap.
